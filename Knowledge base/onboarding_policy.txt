SUPERvity Employee Onboarding Policy

1. Document Submission

New employees must submit all required onboarding documents through the designated onboarding process.

2. Document Verification

HR reviews submitted documents for completeness and authenticity.

3. IT Setup

After successful document verification, employees may receive instructions for setting up their official account, email, and required software.

4. Employee Orientation

New employees may be required to attend an orientation session explaining company policies, workplace practices, and important procedures.

5. Onboarding Completion

An employee's onboarding is considered complete when all required documentation, HR activities, IT setup, and orientation activities have been completed.

6. Assistance

Employees who experience issues during onboarding should contact HR or their assigned onboarding coordinator.
