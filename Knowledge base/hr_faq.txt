SUPERvity HR FAQ
================

GENERAL INFORMATION

1. What are the standard working hours?

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees may have different working schedules depending on their project, team, or business requirements.

2. Is Saturday a working day?

The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday. However, employees should follow the schedule communicated by their respective team or project.

3. How can I contact HR?

Employees can contact the HR department through the official internal HR communication channel or their assigned HR representative.

4. Who should I contact if I have questions about onboarding?

Employees can contact HR or their assigned onboarding coordinator for assistance with onboarding-related questions.

5. What should I do if I face a problem during onboarding?

Employees should contact HR or their assigned onboarding coordinator and explain the issue. HR will guide the employee through the appropriate next steps.


LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE

6. How many paid leaves are available?

Employees receive paid leave according to the company's applicable leave policy. Employees should check the official HR portal or contact HR for their specific leave balance.

7. How do I apply for leave?

Employees should apply for leave through the designated HR or employee portal and follow the approval process defined by their team.

8. Who approves my leave?

Leave approval depends on the organization's approval workflow and the employee's reporting structure. Employees should follow the process configured in the HR portal.

9. What happens if I need emergency leave?

Employees who require emergency leave should inform their reporting manager and follow the applicable emergency leave procedure as soon as possible.

10. How can I check my leave balance?

Employees can check their available leave balance through the designated HR or employee portal.


ONBOARDING

11. What documents are required for onboarding?

Employees may be asked to provide identity proof, educational documents, address proof, bank details, photographs, and other documents required by HR.

12. When will I receive my employee ID?

The employee ID is generally created during the onboarding process after the required documents are submitted and verified.

13. What happens after I submit my documents?

HR verifies the submitted documents. Once verification is completed, the remaining onboarding tasks are communicated to the employee.

14. How long does onboarding take?

The duration of onboarding depends on document verification, completion of required activities, IT setup, and other onboarding requirements.

15. What happens if one of my documents is rejected?

If a submitted document does not meet the required criteria, HR may ask the employee to provide a corrected or replacement document.

16. Can I update my personal information after submitting my documents?

Employees should contact HR or use the designated employee portal to request updates to their personal information.

17. What happens after document verification?

After successful document verification, employees may receive instructions for completing remaining HR activities, IT setup, and orientation.


IT AND SYSTEM ACCESS

18. When will I receive my official email account?

Official account creation generally takes place as part of the IT onboarding process after the required onboarding steps are completed.

19. How do I get access to company systems?

Employees receive system-access instructions from the IT or onboarding team after completing the required onboarding activities.

20. What should I do if I cannot access my company account?

Employees should contact the IT support team or the designated onboarding coordinator and provide details about the access problem.

21. What software do I need to install?

Required software depends on the employee's role and project. The IT or onboarding team will provide instructions about required applications and tools.

22. What should I do if my laptop or system is not working?

Employees should contact the appropriate IT support channel and report the issue.


REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK

23. Can I work remotely?

Remote or hybrid work availability depends on the employee's role, project, team, and applicable company policy.

24. Can I change my work location?

Employees should discuss work-location changes with their reporting manager and follow the applicable company process.

25. Who should I contact about work-location questions?

Employees should contact their reporting manager or HR for questions regarding work location and applicable policies.


SALARY AND BANK DETAILS

26. When will I receive my salary?

Salary payment follows the company's payroll schedule. Employees should contact HR or the payroll team for specific payroll-related questions.

27. How do I update my bank account details?

Employees should update their bank details through the designated employee or HR portal, if available, or contact HR for assistance.

28. What should I do if my salary is not credited?

Employees should first verify their bank details and then contact the appropriate HR or payroll support channel if the issue continues.

29. Where can I find my salary information?

Employees can access applicable salary and payroll information through the designated employee or HR portal.


EMPLOYEE SUPPORT

30. Who should I contact for HR-related issues?

Employees should contact their assigned HR representative or the official HR support channel.

31. Who should I contact for technical issues?

Technical issues should be reported to the designated IT support team or helpdesk.

32. Who should I contact for onboarding issues?

Employees should contact HR or their assigned onboarding coordinator.

33. What if I don't know which department can help me?

Employees can contact HR or the onboarding coordinator, who can direct the request to the appropriate department.

34. How can I get help with company policies?

Employees should refer to the official HR resources or contact HR for clarification.


ORIENTATION AND TRAINING

35. Is employee orientation mandatory?

Orientation requirements depend on the organization's onboarding process. Employees should follow the instructions provided by HR.

36. What happens during employee orientation?

Orientation may introduce employees to company policies, workplace practices, organizational processes, and important employee resources.

37. Will I receive training after joining?

Training requirements depend on the employee's role, team, and project. The relevant team or manager will communicate applicable training requirements.

38. Where can I find training information?

Employees should check the official learning platform or contact their manager, HR, or training coordinator for applicable training information.


ONBOARDING TASKS

39. What are the typical onboarding tasks?

Typical onboarding activities may include document submission, document verification, HR activities, IT account setup, system access, and employee orientation.

40. How do I know which onboarding tasks are pending?

Employees should check the designated onboarding or employee portal for their current task status.

41. Can I complete onboarding tasks in any order?

Some onboarding tasks may depend on the completion of earlier activities. Employees should follow the sequence communicated by HR or the onboarding system.

42. What happens when I complete all onboarding tasks?

Once all required documentation, HR activities, IT setup, and orientation activities are completed, the onboarding process can be considered complete.

43. What should I do if an onboarding task is stuck?

Employees should contact HR or the assigned onboarding coordinator and provide the task details and any relevant error message.


PRIVACY AND SECURITY

44. How is my personal information handled?

Employees should follow the company's applicable privacy and information-security policies when submitting or accessing personal information.

45. Should I share my employee credentials with anyone?

Employees should keep their account credentials confidential and follow the company's security guidelines.

46. What should I do if I think my account has been compromised?

Employees should immediately report the issue through the designated IT or security support channel and follow the instructions provided.
